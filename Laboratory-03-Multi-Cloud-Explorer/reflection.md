# Mission Reflection

At first im confused about this 3 cloud platforms, out of the three cloud platforms, the one that impressed me the most is Amazon Web Services (AWS). I found it interesting because of the wide variety of services it offers and how adaptable it is for different types of users. Whether for small startups or large companies, AWS provides solutions that can easily scale depending on the needs.

Although AWS stood out, I observed that AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform share many common features. All of them provide essential services such as computing, storage, networking, and security. They are also designed to be scalable, reliable, and secure. However, each platform has its own strengths, which makes them suitable for different situations.

Choosing the right cloud provider depends heavily on business requirements. For instance, organizations that already use Microsoft tools will benefit more from Azure due to its seamless integration. Meanwhile, companies that focus on machine learning and data analysis may find Google Cloud Platform more suitable. This shows that selecting a cloud platform is not about popularity, but about how well it fits the needs of the organization.

I now understand how to compare services and recommend solutions based on specific scenarios. In addition, I improved my ability to research and present information using Markdown.
